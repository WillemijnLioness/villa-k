Koukouvayia is the Greek word for owl — wise, watchful, and a little bit magical. Our estate carries the same spirit: a home you inhabit rather than a room you occupy.

Three private residences share the farm's olive groves & walking trails, a deep plunging pool with a toddler's waterfall, star-gazing roof terraces, and endless Aegean views. Where you'll be

Crete, between the sea & sky of Greece.

You'll find us above the small but busy village of Kaloudiana, above the Gulf of Kissamos, nestled in Crete's western mountains. We are a bike ride from the earliest footprints of man, a car ride from the birthplace of Zeus, and within walking distance of both Minoan foundations & the island's extensive & inexpensive public transportation system.

Villa Koukouvayia is surrounded by pristine beaches below and towering mountains behind, yet only 50 minutes from air transport. She invites you to join her for the best of all worlds: everything slower than expected, and everything you could want in a travel experience within a warm embrace & reach.
