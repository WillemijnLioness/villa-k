Welcome to Villa Koukouvayia

A coastal estate,
not a hotel.

'Koukouvayia' is the Greek word for 'owl,' and set above the sea, Villa K embodies this spirit. Wise, watchful and a bit magical, the estate shares the same private spirit: a home you inhabit rather than a room you occupy.

Three private residences share the farm's olive groves & walking trails, a deep plunging pool with a toddler's waterfall, star-gazing roof terraces, and endless Aegean views. Book one house for an intimate escape, combine two for a vacation with others, or take the entire estate for destination gatherings over 20.

Where you'll be

On Crete, between the sea & sky of Greece.

You'll find Villa Koukouvayia nestled neatly into Crete's western foothills, looking down to the small but busy village of Kaloudiana & the Aegean's Gulf of Kissamos. Come, and you'll be a bike ride from the earliest footprints of man, a car ride from the birthplace of Zeus, and within walking distance of both Minoan foundations & the island's extensive & inexpensive public transportation system.

With pristine beaches just below the farm, towering mountains just behind, and major transportation hubs only 50 minutes away, Villa K invites you to join her in the best of all worlds: with everything a bit slower, a bit more beautiful, and all within an easy and warm embrace.
