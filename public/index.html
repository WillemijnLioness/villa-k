Welcome to Villa K

A coastal estate,
not a hotel.

Koukouvayia is the Greek word for owl — wise, watchful, and a little bit magical. Our estate carries the same spirit: a home you inhabit rather than a room you occupy.

Three private residences share an olive grove, a plunging pool with a toddler's waterfall, a star-gazing roof terrace, and endless Aegean views. Book one house for an intimate escape, or take the entire estate for 24.

Where you'll be

Crete, between the sea & sky of Greece.

You'll find us on the Kourthiana Agricultural Road in Kaloudiana, Kissamos, northwest Crete — among the earliest footprints of man, the birthplace of Zeus, and Minoan tombs beside pristine beaches and towering mountains.

Five minutes to local tavernas and beaches. Forty-five to Chania. Everything slower than you expect. Everything further than it looks.

Hosted by the Palola-Harriman family

Heidi, Richard & Logan the dog — on-site, always, and helpful to the degree you'd like.
