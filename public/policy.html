Taxes & Fees

Government Taxes

The Greek taxes we pay on your behalf can be both confusing and high. They include a daily Climate Resilience Tax (described below) that varies depending on the size of the rental space and the time of year, and a rental-value tax ranging up to 45%. To keep your costs down we cap the rental-value tax @ 29%; and to ensure you don't have to worry about any of it, we include every cost into the rate/s we share with you. Simply put, we want you to know all your costs before you decide to stay with us, and will face no surprises after making that choice.

Be sure when comparing Villa Koukouvayia's costs to other options, that they, too, are fully inclusive.

(And of course, if needed, we can itemize all taxes and fees on any bill or invoice.)

Other Policies

Payment, Platform & Other Policies

Currency & Payment

All quoted prices and—unless requested—all charges will be quoted in Euros. USD payments can be accepted at the IMF rate on the day we make your offer, with no surcharges or fees. We encourage direct (IBAN or, in the USA, intrabank) transfers that are easy, safe and free. We can accept payments through credit/payment cards and are happy to do so, but unlike larger third-party sites who spread the inevitable payment-processing fees across all customers, we only apply those fees (3.5% for USD or 5% for other currencies) to the specific card use. Card options and the processing details will be itemized in any offer we send, so that you can be sure to choose a payment method that works best for you.

Payment Plans

Reservations are typically paid in full. Plans of up to 4 payments are available for larger reservations (over €3,000, €4,000 and €5,000, booked 6, 4 and 3 months ahead, respectively). Options & details will be automatically included in any offer we send you.

Security Deposits

A security deposit is included in your payment to hold your booking and to serve as a damage deposit during your stay. Deposits are always returned via bank transfer upon departure, assuming no major issues. A pet damage supplement of €200 per pet applies.

We never confuse 'damage' with 'use.' We expect you to enjoy the home you've rented, and we understand that any gathering of happy people will occasionally drop a glass or two.

Cancellations

We will always work to refund cancelled stays and never charge a fee for this service. We can only do so to the degree we are able to re-book your time.

Check-In & Check-Out

We guarantee check-in from 16:00 and check-out by 10:00. We almost always happily advance or extend those times for you, and we'll always offer an early luggage-drop and/or late luggage-hold so you can enjoy yourself while waiting.

Smoking

We are surrounded by olive groves, and—while beautiful—they are susceptible to fire. Smoking, especially from the April to November fire season, is severely restricted to outside verandas on windless days, and the use of wet ash trays.

Individual Bargaining over Standard Price

Although we understand your individual desire to get a "better" deal than others, please understand that our interactions with each guest must consider all guests. So, we simply do not make individual deals that favor a guest who requests special prices. If we did, we would effectively overcharge guests who correctly trust us to set & publish prices that provide good value and reflect our honest rates. For anyone wondering about our value, we recommend they look at comparable lodgings and read what other customers have said about their experience with us. We post every review written by guests, and we are proud of the consistency with which they agree about the value of staying with us.

Savings over AirBnB, VRBO, and other 3rd-Party Sites

We use the same base prices for all platforms (including our own) but add a surcharge on 3rd-platforms to recover fees & extra taxes we must pay when you book via a 3rd party site. For AirBnb this is 20% and VRBO 19%.

Policy Variations among 3rd-Party Sites and Koukouvayia.com

We strive to make all our policies, fees, discounts, etc. parallel across all platforms, including our own. If you see a difference between platforms, the difference results from 3rd-Party platforms limiting our options within their site listings.

Other Direct 3rd-Party Booking Differences

While booking with us directly will yield considerable savings, there are some features available from 3rd-Party platforms that we cannot provide.

  • Third-Party platforms often put only a 'hold' on your credit-card to cover potential future damages, but we need to have actual funds under our protection once you reserve and until your departure. On the flip side, third-party platforms almost always charge the full reservation value immediately, but as noted above, we can sometimes allow multiple-payment plans. If eligible for partial payments, those amounts and expected dates will be noted on the offer we send you. If not eligible, payment is expected similarly to the third-party sites: in full at time of booking.
  • While, as mentioned above, we will always work to refund cancelled stays and never charge a fee for this service, we can only do so to the degree we are able re-book your time. And while some 3rd-Party sites do charge fees for cancellation, some also guarantee those refunds regardless of whether and when you cancel. Make sure you are comfortable with the policy of whichever platform (including ours) that you will use to book us.

Onsite Ceremony

Event Supplement

Large events burden facilities well beyond nominal lodging use, with deeper facility prep, greater parking needs, and catering & other third-party organizations operating onsite. Most platforms cover these costs by raising their daily pricing across all guests, even for those guests only lodging on site. To avoid such surcharging & keep prices transparent, we simply exclude these costs from our regular pricing, and invoice onsite celebrations a fee equal to three days at the reservation's nominal rate.

The above fee only applies to large celebrations where 30+ people (including 3rd party staff) will be onsite. For smaller events—even special events like weddings 😊—this is waived.
If you/your planner rather than we arrange the event's third-party support (catering, music, etc.) we will add a second (fully refundable) damage deposit to ensure that all providers leave property areas clean and undamaged after the celebration.

Services

Bare Facility or Full Support?

You are welcome to book the bare facility. Please note that Greek law limits us to handing you keys and walking away under this arrangement. Our bare-facility care does include an arrival orientation, daily pool and plant maintenance, and a departure inspection.

If your event would benefit from additional support, our sister company Cretan Destination Retreats can provide:

  • Arrival support and individual guest services
  • Airport transfers and off-campus transportation
  • Catered meals, restaurant arrangements and pantry stocking
  • Cleaning and laundry services at various frequencies
  • Activity guides: hikes, sailing, excursions and bespoke experiences

Typical Service Charges:

  • Third-party arrangements: Invoice +15%.
  • Our own staffing, service or activity guidance: Expenses + €20/hour or €160/day.
With our local contacts, we often secure arrangements at discounts greater than our 15% markup, meaning you may pay less than you would by self-arranging.

Late departures for facilitators who need/want to perform extended wrap-up activities can also be arranged, typically at a discounted rate.
