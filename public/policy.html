Other Policies

FAQs

Currency & Payment

All charges are in Euros. USD payment is accepted at the IMF rate on the day we make your offer, with no surcharges or fees. Credit card payments are available; a 3–5% processing fee is itemised separately so you can choose the best method for you.

Payment Plans

Reservations are typically paid in full. Plans of up to 4 payments are available for larger reservations (over €3,000, €4,000 and €5,000, booked 6, 4 and 3 months ahead respectively). Details are included in your offer automatically.

Security Deposits

A security deposit is included in your payment to hold your booking and to serve as a damage deposit during your stay. Deposits are always returned via bank transfer upon departure, assuming no major issues. A pet damage supplement of €200 per pet applies.

We never confuse 'damage' with 'use.' We expect you to enjoy the home you've rented, and we understand that any gathering of happy people will occasionally drop a glass or two.

Cancellations

We will always work to refund cancelled stays and never charge a fee for this service. We can only do so to the degree we are able to re-book your time. Some third-party platforms guarantee refunds regardless of timing; make sure you are comfortable with the policy of whichever platform you use to book.

Check-In & Check-Out

We guarantee check-in from 16:00 and check-out by 10:00. We almost always happily advance or extend those times for you. We'll always offer an early luggage-drop and/or late luggage-hold so you can enjoy yourself while waiting.

Smoking

We are surrounded by olive groves, beautiful but susceptible to fire. Smoking is severely restricted, especially during the three fire seasons. Wet ashtrays only, outside, on verandas, on windless days.

Onsite Ceremony

Event Supplement

Large events burden our facilities beyond nominal lodging use, with deeper facility preparation, greater parking needs, and third-party organisations operating onsite. To keep this charge clear and transparent, we invoice a separate supplement of three times the applicable day-rate for any onsite ceremony or celebration with more than 30 invitees.

If you arrange third-party event support (catering, music, etc.) yourself rather than through us, a second refundable damage deposit will apply to ensure those providers leave their areas clean and undamaged.

If you lodge your party at VKF but hold the ceremony elsewhere, no event supplement applies.

Services

Bare Facility or Full Support?

You are welcome to book the bare facility. Please note that Greek law limits us to handing you keys and walking away under this arrangement. Our bare-facility care does include an arrival orientation, daily pool and plant maintenance, and a departure inspection.

If your event would benefit from additional support, our sister company Cretan Destination Retreats can provide:

  • Arrival support and individual guest services
  • Airport transfers and off-campus transportation
  • Catered meals, restaurant arrangements and pantry stocking
  • Cleaning and laundry services at various frequencies
  • Activity guides: hikes, sailing, excursions and bespoke experiences

Service charges: third-party arrangements at cost plus 15%; general staffing at €20/hour or €100/full day plus expenses.
