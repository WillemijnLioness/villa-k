Holiday Stays at Villa Koukouvayia Farms:Answers to some FAQs

Finding a Best Fit for Your Holiday Home

Villa Koukouvayia Farms will serve as the perfect holiday home for yourself, your friends & your family during your stay in Western Crete. With three separate residential homes, the estate offers privacy and flexibility for a wide variety of travelers: from couples to young families to groups of friends or families up to 28. Whether you’ve come to for the island’s beaches & mountains, its history, food & wines, or simply to relax among a landscape of green olives & blue sky with those you love, Villa Koukouvayia is ideally designed, built & located to put all of those in your hand.

So, as you consider your stay on the island, let’s talk about how Villa Koukouvayia Farms can serve as the home base and the heart of a great holiday.

Lodging at Villa Koukouvayia Farms

Villa Koukouvayia Farms is a unique property, with four unique residential choices: the Main House, Guest House, Garden Suite and (by combining all three) the entire Estate itself. By design, these are not banks of identical hotel rooms, but each is a private home for those seeking such ambience and they allow you to customize your lodging whether you’re a young couple, small family, or larger friendship & family groups seeking a warm place to gather and share. And while her lodging capacities are unique, their flexibility is the farm’s greatest asset, with strategically placed cabinet bedding easily configured to meet multiple bedding needs.

Calculating Costs

For all whom we host, our goal is to be fair & transparent in our charges, and to avoid surprises once guests decide to stay with us. For instance, Greek taxes paid on your behalf are progressive, confusing and high, including both a daily Climate Resilience Tax & a tax on the value of the rental as high as 45%(!) To keep your costs low we cap the rental tax @ the VAT rate of 24%; and to ensure you don’t worry about any of it, we include every tax in the worksheets below and in any offer we send you.

Simply put, we want you to know all costs before deciding to stay with us.

(And if comparing Villa Koukouvayia’s to others’, make sure they too are fully inclusive!)

Reserving Your Time & Payment Plans

Smaller reservations are typically booked via full payment of the reservation’s cost & security deposit. Larger reservations over €3,000 can qualify for 2, 3 or 4 payments depending on the reservation’s value and the length of time between booking and your arrival. When you submit your inquiry, we will automatically calculate those options & share the details in our offer.

Good to know: 2 payments for €3,000 or more booked over 4 months ahead, 3 for €4,000 or more over 5 months ahead, and 4 for €5,000 or more over 7 months ahead. If a reservation meets only one of the two conditions, the lower number of payments applies (for example, €5,500 booked 4 months ahead allows 2 payments).

Damage/Security Deposits

While not affecting your net expense, once you decide to book with us, your initial payment will include a minimum security-deposit to hold that time against other requests and then later to serve as a damage-deposit during your stay. We set these amounts low to reflect typical guests but will increase them for groups that present a higher likelihood of damage, such as those bringing pets, youth groups, etc.

Of course, assuming no major issues, deposits are always returned via bank transfer at departure.

Currency

As our location demands, we charge in Euros. We accept USD if needed, and unlike banks, card-processors & 3rd-party platforms, we use the simple IMF exchange rate without surcharges or fees.

Cancellation Policy

We always work to rebook cancelled stays, refund all payments to the degree we have rebooked that time, and never charge a fee for this service. While this is often easily done, especially with an early warning of your need to cancel, it cannot be guaranteed.

Payment Methods

We encourage direct (IBAN or, in the USA, intrabank) transfers that are easy, safe and free. We can accept payments through credit cards, but unlike larger third-party sites, we do not hide the inevitable payment-processing fees across all our rates and in doing so charge all customers the 3.5% to 5% fee from which only a few customers benefit. Card options and the processing details will be itemized in any offer we send, so you can choose a payment method that works best for you.

Individual Bargaining vs. Standard Price

Although we understand a guest’s desire either to get a better deal than others or simply meet a limited budget, we feel honor-bound in our interactions with each guest to consider all guests. So we simply don’t make individual deals that favor a guest who requests special prices, because that effectively overcharge guests who quietly trust us to publish prices that reflect our honest rates. Instead, we focus on setting sincerely low rates that allow us to sustain the home and hospitality we offer. And for anyone wondering about our value, we recommend they look at comparable lodgings and read what other customers have said about their experience with us. We post every review written by guests, and we are proud of the consistency with which they agree about the value of staying with us.

Some Final FAQs

Check-In & Check-Out Times

Check-In & Check-Out Times: As always, our goal is to make your stay and your arrival and departure stress-free. While we guarantee arrival check-ins of 16:00 & departure check-outs of 10:00, we can often advance or extend those. There are times, however, when guests depart/arrive close to you, and we must follow those times strictly, so be prepared. Of course, when your flights are at odds with such check-in & check-out times, we’ll always offer early luggage-drop or late luggage-hold so you can do something enjoyable while waiting—either for us to ready your home, or the airline to ready your flight.

Smoking

Smoking: We are set among groves of olive trees, all beautiful, but all susceptible to tragic fires. Smoking is therefore severely restricted, especially during the 3 fire seasons. Wet ashtrays always, always outside, only on verandas, and only on windless days.

Booking via Airbnb or VRBO

Savings over Airbnb, VRBO, and other 3rd-Party Sites

We use the same base prices for all platforms (including our own) but add a surcharge on 3rd-party platforms to recover fees & extra taxes we must pay when you book via a 3rd-party site. For Airbnb this is 20% and VRBO 19%. On direct bookings we cap the rental-value tax at 24%; bookings via third-party platforms carry 35%.

Policy Variations among 3rd-Party Sites and Koukouvayia.com

We strive to make all our policies, fees, discounts, etc. parallel across all platforms, including our own. If you see a difference between platforms, the difference results from 3rd-Party platforms limiting our options within their site listings.

Other Direct 3rd-Party Booking Differences

While booking with us directly will yield considerable savings, there are some features available from 3rd-Party platforms that we cannot provide.

  • Third-Party platforms often put only a ‘hold’ on your credit-card to cover potential future damages, but we need to have actual funds under our protection once you reserve and until your departure. On the flip side, third-party platforms almost always charge the full reservation value immediately, but as noted above, we can sometimes allow multiple-payment plans. If eligible for partial payments, those amounts and expected dates will be noted on the offer we send you. If not eligible, payment is expected similarly to the third-party sites: in full at time of booking.
  • While, as mentioned above, we will always work to refund cancelled stays and never charge a fee for this service, we can only do so to the degree we are able re-book your time. And while some 3rd-Party sites do charge fees for cancellation, some also guarantee those refunds regardless of whether and when you cancel. Make sure you are comfortable with the policy of whichever platform (including ours) that you will use to book us.

3. Supplemental Services & Other Event Costs

Celebrations & events rarely thrive without support services provided on the celebration day itself. More importantly, destination events—where guests arrive at considerable costs and with higher expectations—rarely thrive without providing those guests activities & services during the days surrounding the event that not only reinforce the joys and community at the heart of such an important gathering but avoid increasing your (or your coordinator’s) logistical burden.

As professional retreat and program planners, we specialize in using this beautiful region of Crete—with its historic, culinary, topographic and recreational landscapes—to supercharge the joys at the heart of such gatherings. And while our skills and connections can serve to help you create a dynamic experience for your guests during the entire stay and for everyone at the event itself, for some events—particularly larger-scale weddings—we will recommend you use us only for lodging and non-event logistics, and pay the extra costs to coordinate the event itself with a dedicated wedding-planner—several of whom we can recommend based on our knowledge of their work.

For those hoping for a smaller, more intimate event on a tighter budget—especially if you’re looking to integrate holistic experiences for your group that includes both the event day and the days before & beyond, we’re great partners. We have a lot of experience providing transportation, food, activity &/or reservation services, either holistic or a la carte, and—most importantly—a lot of experience bringing people together to celebrate and have fun. These services include:

  • Arrival Support & Coordination for each guest, including communications, special requests & other individual guest services.
  • Group transportation, including but not limited to vehicle arrangements and acquisition prior to arrivals; airport transfers at arrivals & departures, and off-campus activity, meal & miscellaneous transportation.
  • Food & Beverage Support, including but not limited to catered meals and snack provisions, pantry stocking for internally prepared food provision, and arrangements with restaurants & tavernas for off-campus meals.

Villa Koukouvayia Farms will serve as the perfect place to lodge your group and platform your program, whether its focus be recreational, historical, culinary, health & yoga, or creative and practical arts. As a dedicated retreat center, the estate offers both easy integration between your program’s home and its activities.

Of course, while your program itself might easily highlight your planning attention, let’s talk first about how Villa Koukouvayia Farms can serve to lodge your participants for the days that will platform your program’s experience.

Calculating Costs

Your costs will include two elements: lodging your group and arranging the services that integrate your activities with the destination of Crete to ensure that both are effective. In whatever capacity we might participate, our goal is to be fair & transparent in our charges, and to avoid surprises once you decide to stay with us. For instance, Greek taxes paid on your behalf are progressive, confusing and high. To keep your costs low we cap the rental tax @ the VAT rate of 24%. To ensure you don’t worry about any of it, we include every tax in the worksheets below and will do the same in any offer we share with you. Simply put, we want you to know all costs before deciding to stay with us. (And if comparing Villa Koukouvayia’s to others’, make sure they too are fully inclusive!)

Good to know: The daily Climate Resilience Tax depends on the size of the rental space and the time of year. It is already included in every rate below, and if needed, we can itemize all taxes and fees on any bill or invoice.

2. Supplemental Services & Other Program Costs

Programs rarely thrive without support services provided during the program itself.

As professional retreat and program planners, we specialize in using this beautiful region of Crete—with its historic, culinary, topographic and recreational landscapes—to supercharge the experience at the heart of such gatherings. Transportation, food, activity &/or reservation services, either holistic or a la carte, and—most importantly—integrating all of those into experiences support your goals, while bringing people together to celebrate those goals and have fun. These services include:

  • Arrival Support & Coordination for each guest, including communications, special requests & other individual guest services.
  • Group transportation, including but not limited to vehicle arrangements and acquisition prior to arrivals; airport transfers at arrivals & departures, and off-campus activity, meal & miscellaneous transportation.
  • Food & Beverage Support, including but not limited to catered meals and snack provisions, pantry stocking for internally prepared food provision, and arrangements with restaurants & tavernas for off-campus meals.
  • Cleaning & Laundry Support ranging from simple daily or bi-weekly bathroom and towel service to full bedroom services at various frequencies.
  • Activity Guide &/or Leadership, including but not limited to hikes, outings, Samaria Gorge traverses, historical tours, excursion expertise and guidance, and other customized experiences that fit your attendees’ interests & abilities.

Such services are easily invoiced and at the following general rates:

  • Third-party arrangements at 15% above their invoice.

    Note: With our local contacts, we often garner arrangements discounted more than this % charge, resulting in you paying the same or less for the service than if self-arranged.)

  • General staffing (driving, guided excursions, etc.) at expenses (e.g., gas &/or food) plus €25/hour or €150/full-day.
Good to know: You are welcome to book the bare facility at the costs above. Our bare-facility care includes an arrival orientation, daily maintenance, and a departure inspection. Through Cretan Destination Retreats we can also provide participant enrollment, communications and financial services (at 5% of program value) and program design services for educational and experiential programs.

Reserving Your Time & Payment Plans

Smaller reservations are typically booked via full payment of the reservation’s cost & security deposit. Larger reservations over €3,000 can qualify for 2, 3 or 4 payments depending on the reservation’s value and the length of time between booking and your arrival. When you submit your inquiry, we will automatically calculate those options & share the details in our offer.

Good to know: 2 payments for €3,000 or more booked over 4 months ahead, 3 for €4,000 or more over 5 months ahead, and 4 for €5,000 or more over 7 months ahead. If a reservation meets only one of the two conditions, the lower number of payments applies (for example, €5,500 booked 4 months ahead allows 2 payments).

Damage/Security Deposits

While not affecting your net expense, once you decide to book with us, your initial payment will include a minimum security-deposit to hold that time against other requests and then later to serve as a damage-deposit during your stay. We set these amounts low to reflect typical guests but will increase them for groups that present a higher likelihood of damage, such as those bringing pets, youth groups, etc.

Of course, assuming no major issues, deposits are always returned via bank transfer at departure.

Cancellation Policy

We will always work to rebook cancelled stays, will refund all payments to the degree we have rebooked that time, and we will never charge a fee for this service. While this is often easily done, especially with an early warning of your need to cancel, it cannot be guaranteed

Currency

As our location demands, we charge in Euros. We can accept USD payment if you wish, and unlike all other banks, card-processors and third-party platforms, our offer will reflect the simple IMF exchange rate on the day we make that offer, and held for 7 days beyond, with no surcharges or extra fees.

Payment Methods

We encourage direct (IBAN or, in the USA, intrabank) transfers that are easy, safe and free. We can accept payments through credit cards and are happy to do so, but unlike larger third-party sites, we do not hide the inevitable payment-processing fees across all our rates and in doing so charge all customers the 3.5% to 5% fee from which only a few customers benefit. Card options and the processing details will be itemized in any offer we send, so you can choose a payment method that works best for you.

More Policies

Check-In & Check-Out Times

Check-In & Check-Out Times: As always, our goal is to make your stay and your arrival and departure stress-free. While we guarantee arrival check-ins of 16:00 & departure check-outs of 10:00, we can often advance or extend those. There are times, however, when guests depart/arrive close to you, and we must follow those times strictly, so be prepared. Of course, when your flights are at odds with such check-in & check-out times, we’ll always offer early luggage-drop or late luggage-hold so you can do something enjoyable while waiting—either for us to ready your home, or the airline to ready your flight.

Smoking

Smoking: We are set among groves of olive trees, all beautiful, but all susceptible to tragic fires. Smoking is therefore severely restricted, especially during the 3 fire seasons. Wet ashtrays always, always outside, only on verandas, and only on windless days.

Individual Bargaining vs. Standard Price

Although we understand a guest’s desire either to get a better deal than others or simply meet a limited budget, we feel honor-bound in our interactions with each guest to consider all guests. So we simply don’t make individual deals that favor a guest who requests special prices, because that effectively overcharge guests who quietly trust us to publish prices that reflect our honest rates. Instead, we focus on setting sincerely low rates that allow us to sustain the home and hospitality we offer. And for anyone wondering about our value, we recommend they look at comparable lodgings and read what other customers have said about their experience with us. We post every review written by guests, and we are proud of the consistency with which they agree about the value of staying with us.
