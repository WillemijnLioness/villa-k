Cookies & Privacy

A clear, plain-language explanation of how we handle your information — in full compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Last updated: June 2026

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Villa Koukouvayia Farms, a private holiday property located at Agricultural Road #1, Kourthiana/Kissamos 73400, Crete, Greece. You can reach us through the enquiry form on our website, or by post at the address above.

For the purposes of GDPR, we are the data controller of personal information collected on this site.

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information you voluntarily provide through our enquiry and booking form, which may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your phone number (if provided)
  • Your intended travel dates and guest count
  • Any message you write to us

We do not collect or process payment card details directly — all financial transactions are handled through trusted third-party booking and payment platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, bank transfer) subject to their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the enquiry form is used solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you proceed, to manage your booking. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and provide availability information
  • Prepare and send you a booking offer
  • Communicate about your stay (arrival details, directions, etc.)
  • Meet our legal obligations under Greek and EU law (tax receipts, government registration)

We do not use your information for marketing, profiling, or automated decision-making. We do not send newsletters unless you specifically request one.

4. Our Lawful Basis for Processing

Under GDPR Article 6, we process your personal data on the basis of:

  • Contract (Article 6(1)(b)): to handle your enquiry and fulfil a booking agreement
  • Legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)): guest registration is required by Greek law for all tourist accommodations
  • Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)): to respond to direct enquiries from prospective guests

5. Cookies

This website uses only functional cookies — small text files stored on your device that allow the site to remember your preferences within a session (for example, which tab you last viewed on the policy page). No tracking or advertising cookies are used.

We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any other third-party analytics or advertising platform.

Because we use only strictly necessary functional cookies, we are not required by law to show a cookie consent banner. If we ever add analytics, we will update this notice and add appropriate consent controls.

To disable all cookies, you can adjust your browser settings. This will not prevent you from using this website.

6. Who We Share Your Data With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share limited information with:

  • Email delivery provider (Resend): our transactional email service receives your email address and message content to deliver enquiry notifications to us. Resend's privacy policy.
  • Greek tax and registration authorities: as required by law for tourist accommodation operators.
  • Booking platforms (Airbnb, VRBO): if you book through a platform, that platform's privacy policy governs the data you submit to them.

7. International Transfers

Our email delivery provider (Resend) may process data on servers located in the United States. Any such transfer is conducted under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses, ensuring an adequate level of protection.

8. How Long We Retain Your Data

  • Enquiries that do not result in a booking: deleted within 12 months.
  • Confirmed booking correspondence: retained for 7 years as required by Greek tax law.
  • Guest registration data: retained as required by Greek tourism law.

9. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a resident or visitor in the EU/EEA, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure: ask us to delete your data (subject to legal retention obligations)
  • Restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability: receive your data in a common machine-readable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us through the enquiry form on our website, or write to us at the postal address above. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA) at dpa.gr, or with the supervisory authority of your own EU member state.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. Our website is served over HTTPS. Enquiry data is transmitted directly to us via our email provider and is not stored in any database on our website servers.

11. Links to Third-Party Sites

Our website contains links to external platforms (Airbnb, VRBO, Google Maps). Once you leave our site, this privacy notice no longer applies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

12. Children

This website is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted data to us, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to This Notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised. Significant changes will be noted on the homepage.
