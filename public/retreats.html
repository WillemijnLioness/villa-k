Retreat 01 · March & November

Cretan Collaborative Photography Retreat

A practicum to improve, share, and capture Crete's photogenic landscapes in seasonal light.

Morning and evening field trips in the magic-light hours alternate with sharing sessions — work, technique, and the camaraderie of others on the same path. A day-long seminar and customised 1-on-1 critique with a local professional Greek landscape photographer anchors the week.

What's included

  • Lodging at Villa Koukouvayia Farms among the olive groves
  • Magic-light field sessions at Elafonisi, Old Town Xania, Omalos Plateau, Kallergi Refuge above Samaria Gorge
  • Day-long seminar with a professional Greek landscape photographer
  • Post-production & sharing sessions with supportive peer critique
  • One free day for individual exploration
  • All meals, ground transport, and a great group of fellow photographers

Who should come

Anyone who loves the art and craft of photography and wants to share that passion with others. A digital camera is recommended (not phone-only). Willingness to share, learn, and enjoy company is the only real prerequisite.

Retreat 02 · May & October

Advanced Cretan Lefka Ori Trek

High-alpine hiking at its best and most challenging — a ten-day east-to-west traverse of Crete's White Mountains.

Difficult, non-technical trails for experienced hikers. Each night spent at a great mountain inn with a real meal, with rest days at the pristine beaches of the southern coast — a balance of glamping with tramping.

What's included

  • Askifou Plateau and Asfendou Gorge
  • Samaria Gorge — the uphill traverse from Libyan Sea to Omalos Plateau
  • Gigalos Peak and the Greleskas Observatory trail to historic Koustagerako
  • Rest days at Agios Ioannis, Agia Roumeli & Sougia, with optional supported routes
  • Mountain inns each night for great food and trail-camaraderie

Your guide

Daniel Kolibius, certified mountain trek leader, has been hiking Crete's deeper mountains since age 10 — also an avid Cretan history buff, birder and naturalist. He'll lead and enrich every step.

Limitations

Participants should have appropriate gear and be able to carry a full pack (minus sleeping & cooking kits) on trails covering 0–2,000m+ elevation, often exceeding 10km/day.

Retreat 03 · Early July

Cities of History, Mountains of Myth

Greece's cultural capital plus the ascent of Mount Olympus — a primer of the country's depth.

Four days touring the historical-epoch sites in Thessaloniki — the oldest continuously inhabited city in Europe, with a far broader repository of significant historical sites than Athens. Then south for ancient Dion, an Aegean swim, the mountain village of Litochoro, and an ascent to the luxurious Agapitos Spilios mountain refuge to spend two final days exploring the cloud-laced home of the Gods.

Highlights

  • Three nights at a Thessaloniki hotel in the historic waterfront Ladadika district
  • Preserved Macedonian Tombs, Emperor Galerius' Roman Palace Complex, Byzantine & Ottoman walking tour
  • The bustling Modiano Market and great Old-Town tavernas
  • Guided tour of ancient Dion, Aegean swim, seaside meal at the foot of Olympus
  • Free night in the mountain village of Litochoro
  • Easy hike from refuge to Skala & Skoleio peaks; optional Mytikas summit
  • Program ends in a perfect location to grab a car and continue elsewhere in Greece

Who should come

A perfect primer for anyone on their first visit to Greece, or returning to explore more deeply. Active retirees, parents of young children, and anyone who loves great food, great cities, and dramatic mountains.

Retreat 04 · Early November

The Cretan Harvest Season

Olives, wine, food & fun. The Crete that few visitors ever see.

Not the tourist mecca but the agricultural powerhouse. You'll explore — and participate in — the gathering and processing of Cretan olives, and take home oil you yourself have harvested. Tour an historic organic olive mill and dine at their cliffside estate. Visit two historic vineyards. Mix in a sunrise walk to Seitan Limani's wild pink-sand cove, a tour of Xania's Old Town, and a cooking lesson with a local chef.

Highlights

  • Lodging at Villa Koukouvayia Farms — among the olive groves themselves
  • Joining the annual harvest's community & camaraderie, witnessing oil from tree to table
  • Agia Triada Monastery's vineyard on the Akrotiri plateau
  • Manousakis Winery in the orange- and avocado-filled Moussouron valley
  • Tour and lunch at Biolea's organic olive-oil estate
  • Sunrise visit to Seitan Limani's pink sands and blue water
  • Old Town Chania — Bronze-Age homes through fascist bombings of 1941
  • Cooking lessons from "Do It Like a Cretan" chef Frini
  • Final hike to Milia's hidden village and dinner at the farm-to-fork taverna
  • Optional yoga & experiential wellness with Audrey Paar

Who should come

Foodies, oenophiles, and those who travel to take experiences into their heart rather than selfies on their phone. All ages welcome — restless kids will love the active nature of the harvest itself.
