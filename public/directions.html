Getting Here

We are in western Crete, between the olive groves of Kourthiana and the sea at Kissamos — about 50 minutes from Chania.

From Heraklion Airport

~2 hrs 20 min · ~140 km
  1. Exit the airport and follow signs for Chania / E75 heading west.
  2. Take the E75 motorway west past Rethymno all the way to Chania.
  3. At the Chania ring road, follow signs for Kissamos / Kastelli.
  4. Take the old national road (E60) west from Chania toward Kissamos — approximately 40 km.
  5. Pass through Kissamos town and continue west on the coastal road.
  6. Turn right toward Kourthiana (sign at the junction) and follow the agricultural road uphill. We are at the top on the left.

From Chania Airport

~50 min · ~55 km
  1. Exit the airport and join the coastal road heading west toward Chania city.
  2. Continue through or around Chania on the E60 old national road toward Kissamos / Kastelli.
  3. Drive approximately 40 km west along the coast, past the turn for Nopigia and Drapanias.
  4. Enter Kissamos town and continue west on the coastal road.
  5. Turn right toward Kourthiana and follow the agricultural road uphill. We are at the top on the left.

From Chania Ferry Port

~50 min · ~55 km
  1. The ferry arrives at Souda, east of Chania. Follow signs to Chania city centre.
  2. From Chania, pick up the E60 old national road heading west toward Kissamos.
  3. Drive approximately 40 km along the coast.
  4. Pass through Kissamos town and continue west on the coastal road.
  5. Turn right toward Kourthiana and follow the agricultural road uphill. We are at the top on the left.

From Kissamos Town

~8 min · ~5 km
  1. From the centre of Kissamos, head west along the coastal road toward Falasarna.
  2. After approximately 3 km, look for the signed turn right toward Kourthiana.
  3. Take the agricultural road uphill through the olive groves for about 2 km.
  4. Villa Koukouvayia Farms is at the top of the road on the left. The gate is olive green.

Important Arrival Notes

  • GPS works well in this area, but we recommend saving our Google Maps link before you lose signal on the agricultural road.
  • The road from Kissamos is unpaved for the last stretch — entirely manageable in a standard hire car, even at night.
  • If you plan to arrive late in the evening, let us know in advance. Someone will be here to welcome you.
  • Parking is plentiful and free. Leave your car on the gravel area just inside the gate, or on the upper terrace if you are in the Garden Suite.
  • If you get stuck, call us. We know this road well and can talk you in.
