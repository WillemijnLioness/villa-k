Your Hosts

A home, not a managed property.

Heidi and Richard have been welcoming guests to Villa Koukouvayia Farms since 2019 — though the roots of the property go back much further, to the day they first stood among these olive groves above the Gulf of Kissamos and knew they'd found something rare.

The estate is their home year-round, not a property held at arm's length. They're on-site for arrivals, available for questions, and genuinely happy to share their favourite beaches, their best taverna tables, and the hiking trails that most visitors never find.

To the degree you want company, they're wonderful company. To the degree you want solitude, you'll have it completely.

They also founded Cretan Destination Retreats — their curated programme business — bringing small groups deep into the island's culture: its mountains, its culinary traditions, its ancient sites, and the olive harvest that anchors the whole region each autumn.

🐾

A note about Logan

Logan is their dog. He has been described as "the best dog on Crete" by more than one guest review, a title he takes quite seriously. He is on-site, always, and will absolutely try to sit with you at dinner.

Sister Brand

Cretan Destination Retreats

Heidi and Richard founded CDR to offer what a holiday rental alone can't: curated programmes that take guests beyond the estate and into the full depth of Crete and Greece.

Each programme is designed to be as enriching as it is effortless — photography, alpine trekking, ancient cities, the olive harvest. Or they'll work with you to design something entirely your own.
